## THIRD FINDING — latent data bug
`tracks.plays` was UPDATEd by the play tracker but **never created** in the base schema — play counts silently failed on fresh installs. Fixed: idempotent `ALTER TABLE tracks ADD COLUMN plays / duration` in migration block #55. Also fixed payouts `/history` (component called it; router only had `/`).

## NEW — BST AGENT COMMERCE ENGINE (`/api/bst`)
The profit layer, wired into the money rails above:
- **Agents scan** each creator's catalog and **auto-create sellable products**: commercial license tiers, best-of bundle packs, cover-art prints — priced from real demand signals (plays × base price).
- **Every agent product is a draft. Publishing is one human click** (`POST /api/bst/products/:id/publish`) — per the V12 Constitution: machines stage, humans authorize money-facing actions.
- **Autopilot** (`POST /api/bst/autopilot` per creator + `ENABLE_BST_AUTOPILOT=true` on the service): agents restock the draft shelf every 6h automatically. Publishing stays human.
- Published products sell through `/api/sales/checkout` → Stripe; `/api/bst/earnings` reports real sales, `/api/bst/storefront` is the public shelf.
- Tables: bst_products, bst_sales, bst_settings, bst_agent_runs (migration block #55).

**Honest line:** the engine automates product creation, listing, and storefront — real income additionally requires live Stripe keys (currently placeholders) and buyers. No revenue is invented anywhere.
