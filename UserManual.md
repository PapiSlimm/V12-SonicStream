## 1. Getting Started
To begin, ensure your profile is complete. A high-quality avatar and a compelling bio are essential for building trust with your audience.

## 2. Smart Feed & Discovery
The **Smart Feed** is our proprietary recommendation engine. It uses a weighted algorithm to surface your content to the right listeners.
- **Tip**: High engagement (likes, shares, repeat plays) significantly boosts your visibility in the feed.

## 3. Visual Site Builder
Our drag-and-drop builder allows you to create professional, branded websites without any coding knowledge.
- **Blocks**: Use Hero, Music, Store, Events, and Gallery blocks to showcase your work.
- **Custom Domains**: Connect your own domain (e.g., `artist.com`) via the Globe icon in the builder to establish a professional brand.
- **Responsive Design**: Always check the Mobile view to ensure your site looks great on all devices.

## 4. E-Commerce & Monetization
Sell directly to your fans with our integrated store.
- **Digital Downloads**: Sell high-quality audio files directly.
- **Merchandise**: Showcase physical products with the Store block.
- **Memberships**: Create recurring revenue by offering exclusive content or benefits to your most loyal fans.
- **Unified Checkout**: We handle the processing, taking only a small 4% platform fee.

## 5. Event Management
Promote your live shows and sell tickets directly.
- **Event Blocks**: Add detailed event info including venue, date, price, and ticket availability.
- **Ticketing**: Fans can purchase tickets directly from your site, with real-time inventory tracking.

## 6. Music Distribution (DSP Integration)
Distribute your music to Spotify, Apple Music, and more.
- **Packaging**: We handle ISRC and UPC generation.
- **AI Boost**: Use our AI tools to optimize your metadata and predict viral potential.
- **Royalties**: Track your earnings and payouts directly in your dashboard.

## 7. Maximizing Profitability
- **Direct-to-Fan (D2F)**: By selling directly on your SonicStream site, you keep more of your revenue compared to traditional platforms.
- **Audience Ownership**: Use the Fan CRM to collect emails and build a direct relationship with your audience.
- **Cross-Promotion**: Link your Smart Feed tracks to your Site Builder store to convert listeners into customers.
- **SEO Optimization**: Ensure your site pages have descriptive titles and meta descriptions to rank higher in search results.
